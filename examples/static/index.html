scrollbar

A thin bar of your own along the right edge. Point at the edge or scroll, and it shows; drag it, or press the strip to glide there. It fades 0.6s after scrolling stops.

The browser's own bar is hidden from the first frame — by CSS, not the script — so nothing shifts sideways when the script arrives. On a touch screen, or with no script, the browser's bar stays.

A panel's bar

The list scrolls; the bar sits beside it in the panel's box and names the list: data-pg-scrollbar="#items". The list's own bar is hidden in every browser, Firefox too.

    A pop-up

    The same bar inside a pop-up. While it is open the page is locked, and the page's bar steps away — it sees the page cannot scroll.
